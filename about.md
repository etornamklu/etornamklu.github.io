# About Me

I'm Etornam. I write about things I’m figuring out.

A lot of that is software engineering — Go, Java, Python, TypeScript, Ruby — but not all of it. I also write about work, learning, money, discipline, and the quieter questions beneath how we live and build. I’m interested in systems — technical and human — and how they behave under pressure, over time, and in the real world.

I studied Mathematical Sciences (Actuarial Science) at the University of Ghana and graduated in 2023. After that, I completed my National Service at 4th-IR as an AI Engineer, and I’m currently at Turntabl, building software and learning in public.

Outside of work, I watch anime, read books, and am exploring fitness — swimming, cycling, and longer-distance rides. I’m also learning chess on Duolingo, mostly for the strategy and patience it teaches.
