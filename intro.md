Hey, it’s Etornam

At 23, I’ve come to a realization—boyhood doesn’t last forever. There comes a time when you must make the choice to grow, to take on responsibility, and to become something more. *Odyssey of Becoming* is my attempt to document that journey.

This blog isn’t about having all the answers. It’s about the questions, the struggles, the lessons, and the small victories along the way. It’s about navigating the transition from youth to manhood, from uncertainty to purpose.

I write to understand. I write to remember. And perhaps, in sharing my thoughts, someone else walking a similar path will find something useful too.

This is my journey—an odyssey of becoming.
